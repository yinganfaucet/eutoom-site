Who We Are

A Bathroom Hardware
Specialist for Nearly 20 Years

Quanzhou Guolan Trading Co., Ltd. develops, manufactures, sells and supports bathroom hardware and related products. We serve cross-border e-commerce operators, foreign trade companies and international buyers, with products exported across Europe, North America, South America, Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia and Southeast Asia.

Our philosophy is simple: user-first, quality-first, service-first. Every partner is empowered, every customer is served seriously — and every faucet is finished to a standard we are proud to put our name on.

Our Journey

Milestones

2006

Founded in Wenzhou, Zhejiang. First factory established — the start of our bathroom hardware manufacturing, focused on faucets and shower fittings for export.

2006–2019

Export growth phase. Long-term partnerships built with cross-border e-commerce, trading companies and international buyers across the Middle East and beyond.

2019

Second factory in Quanzhou, Fujian. Capacity expansion in the Nan'an region, consolidating R&D, production and export operations under one company.

Today

Dual-factory supply system. Kitchen & basin faucets, shower systems, smart sensor faucets, valves, floor drains and accessory sets — engineered for project and private-label buyers.
