EUTOO Exhibits at Global Sanitary Ware Expo 2026

EUTOO (Quanzhou Guolan Trading Co., Ltd.) is thrilled to announce our participation in the upcoming Global Sanitary Ware Expo this Fall. As one of the leading manufacturers of bathroom hardware in China, we will be showcasing our newest innovations in water-saving technology and luxury finishes.

What to Expect

Our engineering team has been working tirelessly to expand our product catalog. Visitors to our booth will be able to experience:

  • The 2027 Smart Faucet Series: Touchless technology integrated into minimalist profiles.
  • New Matte Finishes: Expanded durability tests have proven our new PVD coating withstands up to 200 hours of salt-spray testing.
  • B2B Wholesale Solutions: Dedicated managers will be on-site to discuss OEM/ODM opportunities and container pricing for the North American and Middle Eastern markets.

Commitment to Quality

Since 2006, our dual-factory setup in Wenzhou and Quanzhou has allowed us to control every step of the supply chain—from raw brass selection to final CNC machining. This exhibition is not just a product launch, but a testament to our ongoing manufacturing excellence.
