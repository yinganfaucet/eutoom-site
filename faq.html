EUTOOM (Quanzhou Guolan Trading Co., Ltd.) supplies bathroom and kitchen hardware: kitchen faucets, basin mixers, shower systems (including thermostatic and concealed sets), smart sensor faucets, angle valves, floor drains, taps and scuppers, and bathroom accessory sets. A toilets & vanities range is being finalized for launch.

Standard finishes include polished chrome (A), matte black (B), gold / brass (C) and gunmetal / brushed grey (E), plus two-tone combinations such as black-gold (BC) and chrome-gold (CH). Finishing covers polishing, brushed metal and electroplating.
